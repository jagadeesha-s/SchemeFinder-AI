Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)
Department: National Health Authority, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India.
Official portal: https://pmjay.gov.in

Benefit: Health cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation at empanelled public and private hospitals.

Who is eligible: Families identified using deprivation and occupational criteria from the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 database, and other categories added by States. There is no cap on family size or age of members. Senior citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for coverage regardless of income.

How to apply: Eligibility is checked on the official portal using mobile number or ration card details, and eligible people receive an Ayushman card at empanelled hospitals, Ayushman Mitra desks or Common Service Centres. Coverage and implementation can vary by State.

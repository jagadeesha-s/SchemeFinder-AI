Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)
Department: Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India.
Official portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in

Benefit: Income support of Rs 6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2,000 every four months, transferred directly to the bank accounts of eligible landholding farmer families.

Who is eligible: Landholding farmer families, as per the scheme guidelines.

Exclusions (not eligible): Institutional landholders; farmer families in which any member holds or has held a constitutional post; serving or former Ministers, Members of Parliament, State Legislators, Mayors and District Panchayat Chairpersons; serving or retired officers and employees of Central or State Government (except Multi-Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees); retired pensioners with monthly pension of Rs 10,000 or more (except Multi-Tasking Staff, Class IV and Group D employees); persons who paid income tax in the last assessment year; and professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants and architects registered with professional bodies and practising.

How to apply: Farmers can register on the official portal (Farmers Corner) or through Common Service Centres. Land records and a bank account are needed. Aadhaar is used for verification.
